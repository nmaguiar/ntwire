A calm first connection

Everything you need, before you begin.

ntwire is provided by the team that runs the services you need. Your administrator controls what you can access; the app makes that access easy to use.

Get the app

Download ntwire-gui for your computer and open it. On macOS, use an Apple-silicon Mac; on first launch, follow the expected security-warning guidance if prompted.

Have your connection details ready

You will need the server address from your administrator. They will also tell you whether to use an SSH key or your organisation’s SSO sign-in.

Add, connect, open

Add a profile in the settings window, choose Connect, then open the local address shown for the tunnel your administrator granted.

What ntwire does

It creates secure, encrypted paths from your computer to individual approved services. It does not give blanket access to a whole corporate network.

What to expect

The tray icon tells you whether profiles are connected. The local dashboard shows your tunnels, connection state, and helpful details without exposing secret credentials.
