Three small steps

From download to connected

Download ntwire-gui

Choose the version for your computer. The app lives quietly in your menu bar or system tray.

Add your server

Paste the address your administrator gave you and choose the sign-in method your team uses.

Connect and work

Open only the approved local tunnels. The dashboard shows what is connected and where to get help.

Official WireGuard apps

Bring your iPhone or Android.

ntwire works with the official WireGuard clients on iOS and Android. Your administrator can provide a standard profile or QR code with access limited to the services you are approved to use.
